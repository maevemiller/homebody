## Material textures: Poly Haven (CC0)

The other `textures/*.jpg` are the diffuse maps of Poly Haven textures already used by the Blender room builder
(`blender/assets/textures`, fetched by `npm run assets`), resized to 480 px. CC0, public domain:
https://polyhaven.com/license. Linen and plaster are color-corrected in CSS (`filter` in `MATERIALS`), since the CC0
linen is dyed blue. Also from Poly Haven, CC0: `curly_teddy_natural` (bouclé), `cotton_jersey` (linen),
`hessian_380` (jute), `gingham_check`, `wool_boucle` (plaid), `faux_fur_geometric`, `poly_wool_herringbone`,
`marble_mosaic_tiles` (no longer shown). The kilim and block-print photos are cropped to the fabric.
