# Question bank

Last updated: 2026-10-27. Part of the Homebody interior-design reference (see `README.md`).

The questions designers actually ask, grouped by Taste Builder section, with follow-ups that depend on the answer.
Wording is ours, written to be spoken; the substance comes from the designer questionnaires and practice guides
listed at the bottom.

**Legend**
- **Reveals:** what the designer learns, often more than the literal answer.
- **Mode:** **Ask** (open words), **Pick** (a few chips), **Show** (images), or **Ask + Show**.
- **→** a follow-up that appears only after a certain answer ("where they bite").
- Questions marked ◆ are ones designers consider most important for that section.

Most of these are optional depth. A good intake asks maybe a third of them, chosen by what the person says.

---

## 0. Opening (before the sections)

| Question | Reveals | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| ◆ "What made you want to do this now?" | The trigger (move, new baby, partner moving in, WFH, a breakup, finally having money). Sets urgency and emotional stakes. | Ask |
| → (moving) "Is this your first place on your own, or a move up?" | Fresh-start persona; how much they own; budget posture. | Pick |
| → (partner moving in) "Are you two designing it together?" | Two decision-makers; possibly merging two sets of things. | Pick |
| ◆ "What isn't working about the room right now?" | The real problem: layout, light, storage, "it feels like a dorm". Most actionable answer in the intake. | Ask |
| "When it's done, what will be different about your days?" | The outcome they're buying. Often reveals the feeling target. | Ask |

## 1. Function and life

| Question | Reveals | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| ◆ "Walk me through a normal day in this room, morning to night." | Real uses, traffic paths, where light matters when. Surfaces needs a checklist misses. | Ask |
| ◆ "Who's in here most days? Anyone who visits a lot?" | Seat counts, durability, kids and elders. | Pick + Ask |
| → (kids) "How old are they?" | Toddler (soft corners, washable, nothing at grab height) vs teen (hangout space). | Pick |
| → (kids) "Do their things live in here too?" | Toy storage that hides; a kid zone. | Pick |
| "Any pets? Are they allowed on the furniture?" | Performance fabrics vs velvet/bouclé; rug pile; claws. | Pick |
| ◆ "What do you do in here?" (relax, TV, read, work, eat, host, play, music, make things) | The furniture list before style. | Pick (multi) |
| → (work) "Every day, or now and then? Video calls?" | Desk that disappears vs real office; backdrop and face light. | Pick |
| → (host) "Is it dinner around a table, or everyone piled on the sofa?" | Money to the table or to seating. | Show (two images) |
| → (host) "How many people, at most?" | Extra seating, poufs, extendable table. | Pick |
| → (TV) "Is the TV the center of the room, or should it disappear?" | Media wall vs hidden/framed TV. | Show |
| → (reading) "Where do you read now? What would make it better?" | A reading chair and light; book storage. | Ask |
| "Where do things pile up?" | Storage problem (mail, shoes, bags, cables). | Ask |
| "What do you need to store in here?" | Closed vs open storage; how much is "display". | Pick |
| "Do you like your things out where you can see them, or put away?" | Minimal vs collected. One of the best style predictors that isn't a style question. | Show (two shelves) |
| "How's the light? When does it feel best in here?" | Orientation, lamp plan, curtains, paint temperature. | Ask |
| "Is it noisy? Echoey?" | Rugs, curtains, upholstered pieces. | Pick |
| "Do you rent or own? Can we paint, drill, change lights?" | Removable solutions; what can change. | Pick |
| "Is anyone in the house with mobility or sensory needs?" | Seat heights, clear paths, lighting, textures. | Ask (gentle, optional) |
| "Do you cook? Is the kitchen open to this room?" | Sightlines, zoning in open plans. | Pick |

## 2. Aesthetics and taste

Rule: **never ask for a style name.** Show images, ask about feeling, and name the style back to them.

| Question | Reveals | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| ◆ "When you walk in at the end of the day, how do you want it to feel?" (calm, cozy, bright and fresh, energizing, elegant, playful, grounded) | The emotional target; maps to style families (calm → minimal/Japandi; cozy → hygge/organic; elegant → transitional/Deco; playful → eclectic). | Pick 1–2 + Ask |
| → "And when friends walk in?" | Public vs private self. Sometimes "impressive" sneaks in here. | Ask |
| ◆ "Which of these rooms could you see yourself living in?" | First read on style family; strongest single signal. | Show (6–9 rooms, multi-select, plus "none of these") |
| ◆ "And which would you never live in?" | Hard negatives; often sharper than likes. | Show |
| → (picked a room) "What is it about this one?" (the color, the wood, the light, the mess, the plants, that chair) | Which attribute they actually like. Stops us over-reading one image. | Pick chips on the image + Ask |
| "Lighter and airy, or deeper and moodier?" | Value (light/dark) preference, which drives paint, wood tone and textiles. | Show (pair) |
| "Things out on display, or calm, clear surfaces?" | Minimal ↔ maximal axis. | Show (pair) |
| "Straight lines or soft curves?" | Modern/Deco/industrial vs organic/boho. | Show (pair) |
| "Old pieces with a history, or new and crisp?" | Vintage/traditional vs contemporary. | Show (pair) |
| ◆ "Which of these palettes feels most like you?" | Palette family (see `palettes-materials-patterns.md`). | Show swatches |
| → (bold) "Which colors pull you in?" | The specific bold. | Show swatches |
| "Which materials do you love having around?" (wood tones, stone, linen, wool, rattan, brass, black metal, leather, velvet) | Material affinities; echo of keepers. | Show swatches |
| "How do you feel about pattern?" (none, a little, lots) → "Which kinds?" | Pattern appetite and type (stripes, florals, geometrics, block print, checks). | Show |
| "Is there a place that felt exactly right? A hotel, a café, a friend's home, somewhere on holiday?" | People who can't name a style can name a place. Great for the "I'm not sure" path. | Ask |
| "Any restaurants or shops you love the feel of?" | Domino's tip: everyone has a preference whether they know it or not. | Ask |
| "Do you have a Pinterest board or saved photos?" | A ready image set to mine. | Upload / link |
| ◆ "Is there anything you never want to see in your home?" | Hard nos. One saves a dozen wrong suggestions. | Ask + Show |
| "Do you like a lot of different styles?" | Eclectic mixer vs one-look person; need for a unifying thread (one repeated color). | Pick |

## 3. Personal story and meaning

Ask after rapport. Make every one skippable. Toby Israel's "design psychology" builds a client's "environmental
autobiography": the places with the strongest positive memories become the blueprint for colors, shapes, textures
and special objects.

| Question | Reveals | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| ◆ "Where have you lived, or where are you from, that still feels like home?" | Heritage and formative places (e.g. Indian textiles, Copenhagen candlelight). | Ask |
| → (named a place) "What did homes feel like there? The light, the smells, the colors?" | Turns a place into buyable things: a color, a material, a kind of light. | Ask |
| "Is there a home from your childhood, or someone else's, that you loved being in?" | Israel's "highest positive" place. Often a grandparent's house. | Ask |
| → "What do you remember about it?" | A specific object, texture or ritual to echo. | Ask |
| ◆ "Is there one piece everything else should work around? One with a story?" | The hero/anchor piece. | Pick from their things |
| "Is there something small that means a lot to you, a person, a place, a thing, we could quietly nod to?" | One considered nod (a print, a pillow), never a theme. | Ask |
| "What do you collect, or what do you always end up buying when you travel?" | Display needs; a personal palette. | Ask |
| "What do you do on weekends?" | What restores them; hobbies that need space (Designers Today). | Ask |
| "Are you a planned-holiday person or a see-what-happens person?" | Structured vs spontaneous → tidy/symmetrical vs layered/relaxed rooms. | Pick |
| "Is there art or photos you want up?" | Gallery wall, picture lights, framing. | Ask |
| "Are there traditions or rituals we should make room for?" (a puja shelf, a Shabbat table, a Christmas tree spot, tea) | Cultural and spiritual needs, asked respectfully. | Ask |

## 4. Practical constraints

| Question | Reveals | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| ◆ "What budget feels right for this room?" (ranges, plus "not sure, show me what's typical") | Price band for every pick. | Pick |
| → "Is that firm, or is there some give for the right piece?" | Flexibility; where to splurge. | Pick |
| → "If you could splurge on one thing, what would it be?" | Investment priority (sofa, rug, art, lighting). | Pick |
| ◆ "If something has to give, what should we protect: getting it done soon, the budget, the look, or making it personal?" | The 2-of-3 trade-off. | Pick (rank) |
| ◆ "Is there a date it needs to be ready by?" (move-in, guests, a holiday, a baby) | In-stock vs made-to-order; phasing. | Ask / date |
| "Do you want it all at once, or bit by bit?" | Phased plan. | Pick |
| "What's staying?" (already captured in Space Capture) | Keepers as constraints. | Pick from scan |
| "Is anyone else deciding with you?" | Second decision-maker; offer a link for them to react too. | Pick |
| "Do you like to be part of every choice, or would you rather see a finished idea?" | Collaborator vs delegator → how many options to show. | Pick |
| "New, vintage, or a mix?" | Sourcing (secondhand OK? lead times). | Pick |
| "Anything important about how things get in? Stairs, a narrow door, an elevator?" | Delivery constraints. | Ask |
| "Any values we should shop by? Sustainable, made locally, small makers?" | Brand filters. | Pick |

## 5. Closing

| Question | Reveals | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| ◆ "What would make you say 'yes, this is it' when you see it?" | The real success criterion. | Ask |
| "Anything I didn't ask that I should know?" | The thing the structure missed. | Ask |
| Read-back: "Here's what I heard…" with "Did I get that right?" | Corrections; trust. | Show the dossier |

## Asked vs shown: rules of thumb

- **Show** anything visual or where people lack vocabulary: rooms, palettes, materials, patterns, wood tones,
  light vs dark, minimal vs layered, lines vs curves.
- **Ask** anything about life, story, feeling and reasons: routines, places, memories, what isn't working.
- **Ask + Show:** after any image pick, ask "what is it about this one?" with tappable attribute chips.
- **Pick** for facts with few answers: counts, pets, rent/own, budget ranges, dates.
- Give every visual question an **"I'm not sure"** that opens a this-or-that exploration (see
  `learning-taste-from-images.md`) and every story question a **"skip"**.

## Follow-the-bite signals

What a designer hears as "dig here":
- Long or detailed answers, stories, names of places or people.
- Strong words: "hate", "love", "always", "never", "my grandmother".
- Contradictions ("I love minimal, but I have so many books") → ask which wins, or design for both.
- Uncertainty ("I don't know, maybe…") → switch to images.
- A dismissive answer → move on; don't push depth they don't want.
